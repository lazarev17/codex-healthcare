Case

Audit Angel AI: every record audited, not a 2% sample

Clinical leads at GP federations, urgent care providers and care groups were auditing a small sample of records by hand, months after the fact. Rare but serious patterns slipped through.

Define
Sample-based audit misses rare harms and finds problems too late.
Scope
Audit every consultation and care note against NICE, CQC and local standards, with a clinician in control.
Build
Imports from clinical and care system exports, a privacy filter that strips identifiers on the customer's side, rules plus AI that flag risk, and a reviewer who confirms every score.
Run
Agreement with clinicians is measured on every run. Feedback and evidence packs update continuously.

Questions

Before you book

Do you work with our existing systems?

Yes. Clinical, care, scheduling, finance and bespoke systems, through APIs, HL7 or FHIR, database extracts or file feeds.

What if a system has no API?

We get the data out another way, through the database, reports or exports, and build the interface ourselves.

Do you always use AI?

No. We use it only where it beats a simpler fix, and a person checks every output.

How do we buy?

Start with a fixed-price discovery sprint. Each build phase is priced before it starts, and you can stop at any gate.

Who owns what you build?

You do: code, pipelines, data and documentation.

Can you prepare the IG documents?

Yes, if you need them: DPIA, DCB0129 hazard log and DTAC evidence, alongside the build.
