Questions

What people ask before they sign

Will a team outside the NHS understand how we work?

We're a UK company that has built clinical products for NHS providers and passed DSPT ourselves. Engineers spend the first two weeks with your clinicians, not in a ticket queue.

Will we be locked in?

You own the code, the pipelines and the documentation. Handover is part of every plan, whether or not you keep us on.

Is AI safe to use with clinical data?

Identifiers are removed inside your environment, models run under zero-retention terms, and a clinician stays in the loop. If AI is the wrong tool for your problem, we'll say so in the review.

What if the budget runs away?

The review is fixed price. The build is priced per phase, and you can stop at any gate with working software in hand.

Can you prepare the IG and clinical safety documents too?

Yes, if you need them. We can prepare the DPIA, DCB0129 hazard log and DTAC evidence alongside the build, or support your own IG team.

Where is our data processed?

In your environment or on UK-hosted infrastructure, under a data processing agreement where you remain controller.
