The model

What's a forward-deployed engineer?

Looks before it builds

Most teams don't need another tool. They need someone to look at the whole picture, find where time, money and risk leak, and say plainly what to fix first.

Not an agency

An agency takes a spec, builds it somewhere else and hands it over. An FDE sits with your team, on-site or embedded, and learns how your clinicians actually work.

Owns the outcome

Data, integration, AI, the IG paperwork and the handover belong to one small team, so nothing falls between vendors.

Fair questions

What people usually ask us before they sign

"Will a team outside the NHS understand how we work?"

We're a UK company that has built clinical products for NHS providers and passed DSPT ourselves. Engineers spend the first two weeks with your clinicians, not in a ticket queue.

"Will we be locked in?"

You own the code, the pipelines and the documentation. Handover is part of every plan, whether or not you keep us on.

"Is AI safe to use with clinical data?"

Identifiers are removed inside your environment, models run under zero-retention terms, and a clinician stays in the loop. If AI is the wrong tool for your problem, we'll say so in discovery.

"What if the budget runs away?"

Discovery is fixed price. The build is priced per phase, and you can stop at the end of any phase with working software in hand.

"Where is our data processed?"

In your environment or UK-hosted infrastructure, under a data processing agreement where you remain controller. VERIFY hosting options

"How big is the team?"

Usually two or three engineers plus a delivery lead, drawn from our team across the UK, Lisbon and Warsaw. VERIFY
