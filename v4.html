Questions

What people ask before booking

What do you need from us during Proof Week?

One problem, one lead who knows it, a few hours of clinicians' and admin staff's time, and access to the systems or extracts involved.

Do we need a data-sharing agreement before you start?

Not for the first week in most cases. We can start on a de-identified or sample extract, or on the process itself, while the agreements for live data run in parallel.

What happens if it isn't worth building?

You keep the findings and the baseline, and we stop. Most teams find that useful in itself.

Will we be locked in?

No. You own the code, the pipelines and the documentation, and every engagement ends with runbooks and trained people on your side.

Can you prepare the IG and clinical safety documents too?

Yes, if you need them. We can prepare the DPIA, DCB0129 hazard log and DTAC evidence alongside the build, or support your own IG team.
